Version: 2.0.0
Date: 01.11.2026
  Changes:
    - completely reworked the construction-area grow, shrink, and stay algorithm.
    - added an anti-oscillation floor so the construction area locks onto the edge of work instead of bouncing back and forth between too big and too small.
    - added player movement tracking so the construction area adapts faster while walking, running, or flying and unlocks shrinking as you move into new work.
    - normalized grow and shrink speeds across robot counts so small and large equipment setups feel equally balanced and responsive.
    - improved charging handling so robots pausing to recharge do not cause false area expansion or shrinking.
    - optimized equipment swapping to skip replacing roboports when their target radius has not changed.
    - cleaned up and simplified the control script by removing redundant state variables, unused calculations, and duplicate event handlers.
